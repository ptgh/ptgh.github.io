Mac backup

Mac backup is a private tool used only by Paul Tristan to keep encrypted copies of his own files in his own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy

It reads and writes only Paul Tristan's own Google Drive. It collects nothing about anyone, shares nothing with anyone, and sends no data anywhere else. Everything it stores is encrypted on his own computer before it is uploaded.
